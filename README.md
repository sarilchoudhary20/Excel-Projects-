#  FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview
The "FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard" is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyse sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour. It provides business insights through KPIs, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts and Slicers, enabling users to monitor revenue, customer spending, delivery performance, seasonal trends, and product performance in a single dashboard.

---

##  Objectives
- Analyse overall sales performance.
- Track revenue across different occasions and product categories.
- Identify top-performing cities.
- Monitor monthly revenue trends.
- Analyse sales by weekday and order time.
- Provide interactive filtering for better decision-making.

##  Dashboard Features

### Revenue by Occasion
Compares revenue generated across different occasions such as Anniversary, Birthday, Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, and Valentine's Day.

### Top 10 Cities
Displays the top-performing cities based on sales performance.

### Revenue by Weekday
Shows revenue distribution across each day of the week to identify peak sales days.

### Revenue by Category
Analyses sales across product categories including Cakes, Colours, Mugs, Plants, Raksha Bandhan Gifts, Soft Toys, and Sweets.

### Monthly Revenue Trend
Visualises monthly revenue to identify seasonal demand and business trends.

### Revenue by Order Hour
Shows customer ordering behaviour throughout the day, helping identify peak ordering hours.

##  Key Insights
- Generated revenue of **₹35.2 Lakhs** from **1,000 customer orders**.
- Average customer spending was **₹3,520.98** per order.
- Average delivery time was **5.53 days**.
- Sales varied significantly across different occasions and product categories.
- Monthly analysis revealed seasonal fluctuations in revenue.
- Customer ordering patterns highlighted peak business hours and high-performing weekdays.
- Interactive slicers allow users to explore the data efficiently.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard. The dashboard enables users to monitor key metrics, identify trends, compare performance across multiple dimensions, and make data-driven business decisions through an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
